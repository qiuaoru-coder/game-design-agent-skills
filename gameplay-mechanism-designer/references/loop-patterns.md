# Thirteen loop patterns: WHY another round follows

Use a loop pattern when the design needs a repeatable mechanism ring. The labels are mnemonic descriptions of feedback structures, not genres or themes. Choose a primary pattern because it expresses the main repeated tension; add a secondary pattern only when it materially changes the loop.

| # | Pattern | Core loop | Player motive | Central question | Failure risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 充电宝模式 | 储备 → 消耗 → 脱险 → 再补充 | safety, preparedness | Use the reserve now or save it for survival? | Too much insurance erases risk; too little never matters. |
| 02 | 存钱罐模式 | 积累 → 暴露风险 → 选择兑现 → 重新积累 | greed, loss aversion, cash-out | Take the secure reward or risk it for more? | No loss risk means no tension; total loss may over-punish. |
| 03 | 工具箱模式 | 预判问题 → 装备工具 → 遭遇检验 → 调整配置 | preparation, mastery | Which limited tools best cover the next uncertainty? | Universal tools erase choice; unknowable tests become guessing. |
| 04 | 滚雪球模式 | 投入 → 产出 → 再投入 → 加速增长 | growth, compounding, expansion | Reinvest for scale or protect current gains? | Early lead becomes irreversible and removes suspense. |
| 05 | 信用卡模式 | 透支未来 → 当前爆发 → 进入债务 → 偿还恢复 | urgency, calculated gamble | Is the current opportunity worth the future bill? | No repayment becomes free power; opaque debt feels unfair. |
| 06 | 错题本模式 | 失败 → 留下信息 → 调整判断 → 再次挑战 | learning, understanding, conquest | What reusable knowledge did this failure expose? | Unclear failure causes appear random or unfair. |
| 07 | 试菜模式 | 先运行 → 观察反馈 → 局部调整 → 再运行 | tuning, optimization, authorship | What should be added, removed, or reconfigured? | Slow or noisy feedback obscures causality. |
| 08 | 陪练模式 | 读取表现 → 调整压力 → 再次检验 → 继续校准 | calibrated challenge | How can challenge stay demanding but beatable? | Visible rubber-banding harms achievement; endless scaling nullifies growth. |
| 09 | 接力棒模式 | 取得行动权 → 完成本段 → 交接局面 → 下一位继续 | cooperation, responsibility, sequencing | What useful state should this segment leave to the next actor? | Independent stages are rotation, not relay. |
| 10 | 人情账模式 | 做出选择 → 留下记录 → 延迟回应 → 改变未来关系 | responsibility, debt, consequence | What long-term relationship debt does this choice create? | Response arrives too late or without causal signposting. |
| 11 | 行李箱模式 | 完成一段 → 选择保留 → 舍弃其余 → 带入下一段 | preservation, sacrifice, continuity | What deserves to persist and what must be abandoned? | Keep everything means no choice; keep nothing means no continuity. |
| 12 | 猫捉老鼠模式 | 观察习惯 → 生成克制 → 玩家换招 → 系统再适应 | mind games, anti-pattern play | How can the player vary strategy before being read? | Fast counters feel like cheating; slow counters fail to alter play. |
| 13 | 传送门模式 | 系统A输出 → 改变系统B → B产生结果 → 回写A | cross-system surprise, connected world | What genuinely travels both ways between the systems? | One-way reading or weak coupling does not form a loop. |

## Loop construction

1. Identify the result of the current chain.
2. Name what persists into the next decision: resource, information, position, permission, relationship, debt, configuration, opponent model, or cross-system state.
3. Select a pattern whose psychological tension supports the experience goal.
4. Write the loop with explicit carriers on every arrow.
5. Define reset, persistence, escalation, interruption, and exit conditions.
6. Check that the next round starts with a meaningfully changed decision rather than simple repetition.

## Selection cues

- Safety and recovery: 充电宝.
- Risk and cash-out: 存钱罐.
- Pre-run preparation: 工具箱.
- Compounding growth: 滚雪球.
- Borrowed power and debt: 信用卡.
- Knowledge from failure: 错题本.
- Iterative tuning: 试菜.
- Adaptive challenge: 陪练.
- Turn or role handoff: 接力棒.
- Delayed social consequences: 人情账.
- Cross-stage inheritance: 行李箱.
- Opponent adaptation: 猫捉老鼠.
- Bidirectional cross-system feedback: 传送门.

Source basis: 六边形老闪《一口气看懂13种游戏模式》v4, which summarizes 84 mechanism rings into 13 mnemonic patterns.
